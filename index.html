FAQ

Questions shippers ask us first.

Which routes do you cover?

We run trucking across Indonesia and connect other islands through sea and air forwarding. Send your origin and destination and we will confirm availability and lead time for that lane.

What truck types are available?

Our fleet includes wingbox trucks, trailers and CDD long units, available as dedicated capacity or on call.

Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Every unit carries GPS, and our control tower shares live position and milestone updates from pickup to delivery.

Do you clear customs if another company ships my cargo?

Yes. Our PPJK team handles import and export clearance on its own, whether or not we move the cargo.

How flexible is warehouse storage?

Storage plans run daily, weekly or monthly, so you only pay for the space and time you actually use.

What do you need from me for a quote?

Origin, destination, cargo type, estimated weight or volume and how often you ship. Send it on WhatsApp or email and we will reply with a written quote.
